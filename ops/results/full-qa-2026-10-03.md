## 1. Stubbed full-pipeline run on real Redis 7 (CI job `pipeline-e2e` in `ci.yml`)

`scripts/qa/pipeline-e2e.ts`, one process per scenario, Redis flushed between them, about 6 minutes in
parallel with the other jobs. It reuses the 20.29 load-test harness (merged from `origin/p20-load-test`, which
had no PR yet: `src/lib/studio/load-test/` fake providers, guard, local storage, sample media) and the real
workers, router, cost guard, quality gate (real FFmpeg) and publishers. The only new pieces are
`load-test/qa-faults.ts` (a fault wrapper for a simulated provider and a fetch that answers a social platform
with the 401 a dummy key gets) and the scenario script. No paid API, no network: `localOnlyFetch` and the
fake-provider guard stay on. Overlap to note: when `p20-load-test` merges, git sees the same commits; the
load-test workflow and this job both exist and are independent.

| Scenario | What it proves |
|---|---|
| `full-brief` | brief, ideation, script (7 shots), shots, voice, music, composition (Shotstack stub), one render per format, quality gate passed, **auto-approved**, publish with a dummy TikTok key (**the platform answers 401: `tiktok/needs_reconnect: access_token_invalid…`, no stack, no raw error**), then an analytics poll of a stand-in published post stores hour and day buckets with the polled numbers |
| `slideshow` | 8 slides (hook card, five photos found by library, stock or generation, a quote, closing card), no AI clip bought, composition, render, quality gate passed, READY_FOR_REVIEW |
| `failover-429` | Seedance answers 429 on every submit: 5 refusals, no Seedance clip, **4 Kling clips**, video reaches review |
| `failover-credits` | Seedance out of credit: the same call fails over to Kling at once, Seedance is held out after its first refusals (2 in total), video reaches review |
| `cost-cap-project` | a 40p project budget: `cost_cap_paused: project reached 90% of its budget (£0.41 of £0.40)`, no render, nothing submitted after the pause |
| `cost-cap-org` | a 60p organisation daily cap: `cost_cap_paused: organisation daily cost cap reached…`, no render, nothing submitted after the pause |

Findings while building it (no product bug): the harness sample render is 30 s, so a slideshow must total
about 30 s for the quality gate's duration check (a slideshow is at most about 13 s of default slides; the
scenario uses the 4 s photo maximum and a 5 s quote); and a run may generate only five photos, the sixth
becomes a text card (by design, 20.26). Not covered by the stub: real publishing and real analytics (PROD),
and the thumbnail step logs a warning because the fonts host is the app, which does not run in this job.

## 2. Accessibility (axe-core)

`@axe-core/playwright` 4.13.0 (exact, dev). `e2e/qa/pass2-a11y.spec.ts` scans WCAG 2.0/2.1 A and AA on every
public page (home, pricing, sign in, sign up, forgot password, six legal documents), every signed-in page
(19 workspace and account pages, three project states, a publication analytics page) and every Admin Centre
tab, in the light and the dark theme. Serious and critical violations fail it; every violation is attached.
It runs after `library-templates.spec.ts` on purpose: visiting `/library` first filled the library read cache
with an empty list, which hid the specs' directly-seeded rows.

Found and fixed (all serious, colour contrast unless noted):

1. Brand vermilion text and buttons were 4.0 to 4.1:1 on the cream background (wordmark, sign-in button,
   tinted badges): `--primary` darkened to L 0.53.
2. `text-destructive` on its 10 to 12% tint was 3.9 to 4.3:1 in both themes: light `--destructive` L 0.5,
   dark L 0.72.
3. `text-success` on its tint was 3.3:1: `--success` L 0.48.
4. Admin Centre tab triggers (`text-foreground/60`) were 4.48:1: 72%.
5. Landing page "take" label on the dark slate was 3.4:1 (2.6:1 in dark): fixed per theme.
6. Calendar day numbers outside the month were 3.2:1 in dark (`text-muted-foreground/60`): full muted colour.
7. `aria-label` on a `div` (every loading skeleton): `Skeleton` now has `role="status"` when labelled.
8. Cost report: a `meter` as a direct child of a `dl` row: moved inside its `dd`.
