# Cookie Policy

**Last updated: 30 September 2026**

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used by PostMind Studio (the **Service**), provided by Postmind AI Ltd. It should be read with our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy).

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, work in a comparable way. Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR), a website may use cookies that are **strictly necessary** to provide a service you ask for without your consent, but needs your consent for any others.

## 2. The short version

- We only use cookies and storage that are strictly necessary for the Service to work or that remember a choice you made.
- **We do not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies**, we do not use third-party analytics or advertising scripts, and we do not show a cookie banner because we have nothing to ask your consent for.
- Payment, "Continue with Google" and social-media connection pages are run by those companies on their own websites, under their own cookie policies.

## 3. Cookies set by the Service

On our secure website the sign-in cookies' names begin with `__Secure-`, which tells your browser to send them only over HTTPS.

| Name                            | Purpose                                                                                                                  | Duration                                                      | Type                                       |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| `__Secure-studio.session_token` | Keeps you signed in and identifies your session. Signed and not readable by page scripts.                                | 14 days from your last visit (renewed as you use the Service) | Strictly necessary                         |
| `__Secure-studio.session_data`  | A short-lived signed copy of your session, so the Service does not have to look it up on every request.                  | 60 seconds                                                    | Strictly necessary                         |
| `__Secure-studio.two_factor`    | Remembers that you have entered your password and are part-way through two-step verification.                            | 10 minutes                                                    | Strictly necessary                         |
| `__Secure-studio.trust_device`  | Set only if you tick the option to trust this device, so you are not asked for a two-step code on it again.              | 30 days                                                       | Strictly necessary (set at your request)   |
| `__Secure-studio.admin_session` | Used only by our support staff: holds the staff member's own session while they view the Service as a user to help them. | While the support session lasts (up to 30 minutes)            | Strictly necessary                         |
| `studio.locale`                 | Remembers the language you chose.                                                                                        | 1 year                                                        | Strictly necessary (remembers your choice) |

## 5. Third parties

5.1 **Stripe.** When you pay, start a trial or manage your subscription, you are taken to pages hosted by Stripe, which sets its own cookies, including for fraud prevention. See Stripe's cookie policy at [https://stripe.com/legal/cookies-policy](https://stripe.com/legal/cookies-policy).

5.2 **Google and social platforms.** "Continue with Google" and connecting TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, X or LinkedIn take you to those companies' own sign-in and permission pages, which set their own cookies under their own policies. The Service does not place their cookies or tracking pixels on our pages.

5.3 **Fonts in the video editor.** When you preview on-screen text in the video editor, your browser loads the chosen font from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address and browser details. Google states that the Google Fonts service does not set cookies. See [https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).

## 6. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the cookies in section 3, you will not be able to sign in or use the Service. Deleting `studio.locale` resets your language choice. Guidance for common browsers is at [https://ico.org.uk/for-the-public/online/cookies/](https://ico.org.uk/for-the-public/online/cookies/).

## 7. Changes and contact

If we change the cookies we use, we will update this page and its date. If we ever want to use cookies that are not strictly necessary, we will ask for your consent first. Questions: support@postmindai.pro.
