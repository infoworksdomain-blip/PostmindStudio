## 6. Plans, trial and fair use

6.1 **Plans.** Our self-service Plan is priced per Channel. You choose how many Channels you pay for (from 1 to 6) and how often you pay: weekly, monthly or yearly. Each Channel includes 8 short HD videos a month. On weekly billing, each Channel includes 2 videos a week instead. On yearly billing, each Channel includes 96 videos a year, released as 8 videos at the start of each calendar month. Quick posts count as a quarter of a video, so each video included can instead be used for up to 4 quick posts (clause 6.3). Videos you do not use in a week or month do not carry over. Long videos are not included in the self-service Plan. The prices, and the seats, businesses, storage and features that come with the Plan, are shown on our [pricing page](/pricing) and in the Service at the time you buy or renew. The Enterprise Plan is quoted individually and set out in an Order Form.

6.2 **Trial.** New organisations may be offered one free trial, normally 14 days. You must give a valid payment card to start the trial. The trial includes 5 short videos (long videos are not included). Only one trial is available per organisation and per payment card; if we detect a repeat trial, the trial ends at once and the first payment is taken. **Unless you cancel before the trial ends, your subscription starts automatically and your card is charged the Fees for the number of Channels and the billing period you chose.** If the trial ends and the first payment cannot be taken, you cannot create or publish content until you subscribe.

6.3 **Video allowance and usage limits.** Your Plan's videos are counted per week on weekly billing and per calendar month (UTC) on monthly and yearly billing. Every video you start counts, including videos still being made. Carousels, slideshows, wall-of-text videos and hook + demo videos (**quick posts**) each count as a quarter of a video, a UGC actor video counts as two videos, and every other video counts as one video. To keep the Service reliable and to prevent misuse, we also apply internal limits to AI usage (per day, per month and per video), and the Service limits how many AI-generated clips a video uses, filling other shots with your own images, stock images or animated text cards. When your videos for the week or month are used up, or one of these limits is reached, generation pauses until more videos are included at the start of the next week or month, until you add a Channel or buy a Video Pack, or, for a single video, until you choose to carry on making it in the Service. Work already done is not lost when generation pauses. We tell you in the Service and by notification as you approach and reach your allowance.

6.4 **Fair use.** Where a Plan is described as unlimited, it is subject to fair use: use consistent with a single business's genuine marketing needs, within the usage limits in the Plan or Order Form. We may contact you, and if needed limit use, where your usage is far outside normal patterns for your Plan.

6.5 **Video Packs.** While you have a Plan, you may buy one-off Video Packs of extra HD videos (currently 5 or 15 videos) at any time. Video Pack videos can be used on any Channel, are counted in the same way as the videos in your Plan (clause 6.3), are used only after the videos included in your Plan for the week or month, and are valid for 3 months from purchase. They cannot be exchanged for cash and are not transferable. Unused Video Pack videos expire at the end of their validity and are not refundable, except where the law requires; they are lost if your Organisation is deleted.

6.6 **Channels.** You may connect more Connected Platforms than the Channels you pay for, but the Service publishes only to as many platforms as your Plan has Channels: the platforms your Organisation connected first. To publish to another platform, add a Channel (or disconnect a platform you no longer use).
